🚀 Quick Jump for Judges / Evaluators

🎯 Core SIH Principles Implemented

  • Low-Digital-Literacy UX: Minimum 56px touch targets, icon-first, high contrast.
  • Bilingual Architecture: Instant one-tap toggle between Hindi & English across all screens.
  • Voice-First Workflow: Speech-to-text with Gemini + audio TTS readback.
  • AI Studio Enhancer: Eliminates workshop clutter, balances lighting, 1:1 e-commerce ready.
  • Fair-Trade Pricing Engine: Calculates fair living wage based on materials, labor hours & 35+ Indian handicraft benchmarks.
